## Long-Term Vision
Transform the platform from an internship portal into a complete AI-powered career ecosystem.

### Students should be able to:
* Build and optimize resumes
* Practice interviews
* Improve portfolios
* Analyze GitHub profiles
* Track career growth
* Discover internships and jobs
* Receive AI-powered coaching
* Learn missing skills
* Connect with mentors and recruiters

### Recruiters should be able to:
* Discover candidates using AI
* Rank applicants intelligently
* Generate job descriptions
* Schedule interviews
* Analyze applicants automatically
* Manage hiring pipelines efficiently

### Universities should be able to:
* Track internship placements
* Monitor student employability
* Generate reports
* View department analytics
* Manage employer partnerships

**The goal is to evolve into a complete AI-powered career development and recruitment platform rather than just another internship website.**
